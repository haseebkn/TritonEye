# Native NL training opportunities v0.1.0

Two original reserved Sentinel-1 A HH/HV acquisitions, 29 April 2019 and
27 June 2020, both with frozen training roles and distinct geographic/acquisition
groups. Raw imagery remains local under ignored `data/source_supported/`.

The collection has two 384x384 areas and one provisional source-compatible
iceberg hypothesis. The other area has no distinct boxed target and incomplete
source-uncertainty coverage; it is not a negative example. Entire areas and all
objects are ignored for accuracy and fitting. No independently reviewed truth,
confirmed physical identity, exhaustive labels or usable training admission is
claimed. Prior project/source exposure and AI annotation are disclosed.

The first pass, subsequent source reconciliation, private source audit,
calibration/crop receipts and ignore masks are hash-bound in collection.json.
The native 75x75 research context does not grant class or training approval.
The original locked test, quarantine, previous datasets and masks are untouched.

See [the annotation report](../../../docs/TRAINING_NATIVE_ANNOTATION.md) for
the protocol, source attribution, uncertainty/corroboration limits and replay
commands. This is a separate experimental collection, not a replacement benchmark.
